# Steroscopic Video

Stereoscopic video is a special kind of video that shows two pictures at the same time — one for each of your eyes. Just like your eyes see the world from slightly different spots, the video gives each eye its own view. When you watch it using something like a cardboard VR viewer, your brain puts the two views together and it feels like you’re really inside the video, with things popping out or stretching far away.

Creating stereoscopic video means making those two different views on purpose. You can use two cameras side by side, or a computer to make two versions of the same scene — one for the left eye and one for the right. Then, the two views are placed side by side in one video, so your eyes get the right view when you watch it in VR. It’s like filming the world the way your eyes already see it.
